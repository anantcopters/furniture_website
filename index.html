About Sethiya Industries

Manufacturing durable furniture, delivered across India.

Sethiya Industries was built on a simple premise: furniture should be able to take a beating and keep working. What started as a small workshop has grown into a full manufacturing facility producing steel, wooden and hospital furniture for buyers across India.

Every piece is fabricated on-site — from steel cutting and welding to wood finishing and powder-coating — so quality control never leaves the building. That in-house control is what lets us take on custom government and institutional orders with confidence.

Our commitment is straightforward: furniture that holds up to daily institutional use, delivered on schedule, at a price that reflects direct factory manufacturing rather than a retail markup.
